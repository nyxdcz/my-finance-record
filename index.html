          Device recovery guidance

          Use an external recovery bundle before replacing a device or clearing browser data

          Replacing a device
          1. Export a recovery bundle from the old device.
          2. Install V12 from the same HTTPS site on the new device.
          3. Import and review the bundle.
          4. Confirm record counts and totals before merging or replacing.
          5. Rename the new device and mark the old one as replaced.
          Lost device or cleared browser data
          1. Open the newest external recovery bundle.
          2. Review its source device and backup date.
          3. Import it; V12 creates a pre-import recovery snapshot first.
          4. PDF packs must be restored separately because they remain optional local files.
          5. Persistent storage reduces eviction risk but is not a backup.

          How the calculation works

          Available money is the total of the account balances included by your Savings settings. Monthly expenses includes all selected-month records marked “Include in calculated totals,” whether paid or unpaid. Outstanding expenses includes only the unpaid portion of those records. Money remaining uses the page context: Dashboard and Reports subtract monthly expenses, while Budget & Expenses subtracts outstanding expenses. Total savings and Net cash flow remain separate.
