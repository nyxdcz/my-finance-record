# V13.0.9 Phone UI & Account Balance Refresh Validation

## Scope
- Phone top bar: contextual Add first, Sync second, More last; one SVG plus only.
- Phone Budget & Expenses: compact two-column summary cards with full-width Outstanding and Money remaining rows.
- Phone Projects: compact mobile cards, concise facts/statuses, primary action + More menu.
- Desktop/iPad Projects: no action-text overlap.
- Account reconciliation: saved balance immediately refreshes account cards and dependent totals from the append-only ledger.

## Responsive targets
- 1440 × 900 MacBook
- 1280 × 800 MacBook
- 1024 × 768 iPad
- 393 × 852 iPhone
- 360 × 800 narrow phone

## Preserved
- Finance Schema 12, Cloud Schema V3, ledger history, reconciliation history, project revisions, payments, savings logic, encrypted sync, and stored records.
