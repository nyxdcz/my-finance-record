# V12.18.8 Settings Version History Validation

- Appearance is removed from Settings navigation and panels.
- Accounts & Savings is the default Settings category.
- Legacy `settings=appearance` links fall back to Accounts & Savings.
- The top-right theme button still cycles System, Light, and Dark preferences.
- Advanced contains About & technical details followed by Version History.
- The newest release is expanded by default and older entries are collapsible.
- Version summaries are embedded and work offline.
- Desktop, tablet, phone, light, and dark layouts remain readable without horizontal overflow.
